Blog: Where to Find Loyal Customers Who Spend More
Published: 3 August 2026

---

You might assume your best customers are defined by who they are — their age, income, or location.

The data says otherwise.

We analysed 100,000 customer records from a Malaysian online retailer. Age, gender, and location had almost no effect on loyalty. What mattered was one thing: which channel brought them in.

Paid search customers had the highest loyalty score at 1.582. Referral customers followed at 1.579. Social media customers scored the lowest at 1.566.

That gap looks small. Across thousands of customers, it compounds into a real difference in retention, repeat spend, and lifetime value.

Paid search works because customers arrive with intent — they searched for something specific and found you. Referral works because trust is already built before the first purchase. Social media brings browsers, not buyers. They arrive because a post caught their eye. When the moment passes, so does the engagement.

The organic and email segment was the largest in the entire base at 45,103 customers — cost-efficient to maintain and more loyal than social media.

If your marketing budget is still allocated the way it was two years ago, it might be worth asking: which channel actually keeps the customers you acquire?
