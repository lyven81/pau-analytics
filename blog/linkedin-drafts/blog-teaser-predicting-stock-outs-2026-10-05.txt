Blog: Predicting Stock-Outs Before They Happen: What Inventory Data From a Malaysian Minimarket Reveals
Published: 5 October 2026

---

A minimarket owner in Selangor kept running out of stock on the same shelves, month after month.

His total inventory count looked fine. His reorder threshold was set. He had already raised it once. The shelves still went empty.

Pau Analytics looked at 18 months of inventory records across his three outlets — 450-plus SKUs. The problem was not how much stock he had. It was when it arrived.

73% of his stock-outs were hitting the same 12 products. And those 12 products were running out on the same three days every month — the three days before payday, when demand was running 2.8 times the weekly average.

He had a timing problem. He had been trying to solve it with a quantity fix.

Two adjustments came out of the data. Weekly restocking on the 12 high-velocity SKUs instead of fortnightly. A standing month-end buffer at the one outlet closest to the workers' hostels, where 58% of the stock-outs were happening.

Total stock across the chain barely changed. The distribution changed. Stock-outs on those 12 items fell 64% in three months.

The full breakdown — including how to identify your own high-risk SKUs and why your weekly stock report may be hiding the problem — is in this week's blog post.
