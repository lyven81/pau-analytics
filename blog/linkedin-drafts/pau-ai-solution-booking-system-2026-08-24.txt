Your best customers are trying to book at 10pm. Nobody answers.

Service business owners know this pattern. Evenings and weekends are when customers finally have time to sort out appointments — hair treatments, spa bookings, tuition slots, workshop repairs. They message your WhatsApp, they check your Instagram, they look you up on Google. All after hours.

But most service businesses stop responding the moment the owner goes home.

The Pau AI Booking System is built to handle this gap.

A customer messages at 10.30pm asking for a slot next Saturday. The system checks your live calendar, confirms what is available, applies your pricing rules, and sends a booking confirmation via WhatsApp — while you are asleep. No missed message. No back-and-forth to sort out in the morning. Just a confirmed booking waiting when you wake up.

What it handles automatically:

Real-time availability checks so no slot gets double-booked. Pricing rules for peak hours, off-peak slots, and public holidays applied without any manual input. Booking confirmations sent instantly via WhatsApp. Day-before reminders to reduce no-shows.

Salons, spas, clinics, tuition centres, and repair workshops across Malaysia use this to stop losing customers who contact after hours and never hear back.
