Mr. Krishnaswamy runs a neighbourhood pharmacy in Ampang. One pharmacist, two assistants, a WhatsApp number that starts ringing before the shutters go up.

By 10am every morning, his team has already answered the same eight questions thirty-something times.

"Do you have [medication]?" "What time do you close on Sunday?" "Can I buy this without a prescription?" "How much is Panadol Forte?"

By the time the real consultation work starts — advising a patient on drug interactions, checking dosage for an elderly customer, handling a prescription refill — the team is already behind.

Pau AI's Expert Chatbot now takes the first contact.

When a customer messages the pharmacy WhatsApp, the chatbot:
• Checks availability across 400+ listed products instantly
• States current pricing for OTC medications
• Confirms whether a prescription is required before the customer makes a trip
• Answers opening hours, location, and service questions around the clock

Complex queries — drug interactions, refill discussions, anything that needs professional judgement — route directly to the pharmacist.

Since setup, Mr. Krishnaswamy's team handles 60% fewer repetitive incoming messages during peak hours. The pharmacist spends more time on actual patient care. The WhatsApp line that used to create a queue now clears itself before the team is even aware of it.

The same hour that disappeared into repeated questions is back — and it goes to work that actually needs a person.
