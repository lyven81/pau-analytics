Category: booking-system / dental-clinic
Published: 7 November 2026

---

Dr Lim runs a two-chair dental clinic in Ampang. Two dentists. One receptionist. Around 30 appointments a week.

The problem was not the dentistry. It was the phone.

Patients called during lunch, after 6pm, and on Sundays — the exact times the receptionist was not there. WhatsApp messages sat unanswered until the next morning. By then, some patients had already booked elsewhere. Others simply gave up.

Dr Lim estimated she was missing 4 to 5 confirmed bookings per week. At an average appointment value of RM180, that was up to RM900 in lost revenue every week — not from bad reviews, just from an inbox that nobody monitored after hours.

She connected the Pau AI Booking System to her WhatsApp number.

Now when a patient messages at 9pm asking for a scaling appointment, the system:

- Checks her live calendar for available slots in the next 7 days
- Confirms which dentist is available for that appointment type
- Sends a confirmed booking via WhatsApp with the date, time, and payment reminder
- Sends a day-before reminder automatically to reduce no-shows

Within the first month, Dr Lim's after-hours booking rate — appointments confirmed outside of 9am to 6pm — went from near zero to 22% of her weekly total. Her no-show rate dropped from 18% to 9% after the reminder messages went live.

Her receptionist now spends the first 30 minutes of each day reviewing confirmed bookings rather than chasing missed calls.
