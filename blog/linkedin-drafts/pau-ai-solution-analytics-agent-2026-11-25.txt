Your sales data is sitting there. Your POS has months of records. But getting an actual answer from it still takes you two days, a spreadsheet you half-understand, or a request to someone who comes back with something that answers the wrong question.

That is not a data problem. That is an access problem.

Pau AI's analytics agent connects to your data — POS records, inventory logs, delivery platform exports, HR files — and lets you ask questions in plain English or Mandarin.

No formulas. No dashboards built by someone else. No waiting.

A kopitiam owner asked: "Which items sold the least in the past two weeks?" The agent flagged 8 items in seconds. He pulled them off the menu. Prep time shortened. Wastage dropped.

A restaurant owner asked: "Which day of the week is dragging down my weekly revenue?" The answer came back pointing to Tuesday lunch — consistently underperforming for four months. He shifted his midweek promotion there. Revenue for that slot improved within six weeks.

The questions are always specific. The data is always already there. The gap is just having a fast way to ask.

Built for F&B operators, retailers, and service businesses with data in their systems and no easy way to get answers from it on the spot.
