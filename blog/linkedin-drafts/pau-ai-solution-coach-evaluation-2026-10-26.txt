You hired four coaches. One of them is quietly losing you students every month.

You cannot see it in the revenue line. Monthly income looks the same. But look at renewals, dropout rates, and progression scores by coach — and a pattern emerges that no one talks about.

Pau AI's coach evaluation system tracks what matters for coaching businesses: not just session counts, but outcomes.

For every coach in your program, it monitors:

- Student renewal rate: what percentage re-enroll at the end of each term
- Dropout timing: when students leave and whether the timing clusters around a specific coach
- Progression scores: are students hitting milestones at the expected pace
- Parent or student satisfaction ratings: week-by-week, not just end-of-year surveys

One tuition centre used it to spot that one coach had a 94% renewal rate while another was at 58% — with the gap widening each term. The centre's overall renewal rate looked fine at 79%. The problem was invisible in the aggregate.

Once the pattern was visible, the response was simple: peer observation, shared lesson planning, and one-to-one coaching support. The underperforming coach's renewal rate rose to 74% in six months.

Built for tuition centres, fitness studios, sports academies, and any business where the quality of individual coaches directly drives retention.
