Your front counter answers the same 20 questions every day.

"What are your opening hours?" "Do you have vegetarian options?" "How much does it cost?" "Can I book for Saturday?"

At a quiet moment, those questions take seconds. During a lunch rush, a Friday evening peak, or a Saturday morning queue, every one of them pulls a staff member away from something else.

Pau AI's Expert Chatbot handles the questions your team answers on repeat — 24 hours a day, in English and Malay, through WhatsApp or your website.

It knows your menu, your services, your pricing, your policies, and your hours. A customer sends a question at 11pm on Sunday. The chatbot answers immediately. When they're ready to book or buy, it passes them to a staff member or booking flow.

It works for restaurants, clinics, training centres, workshops, pharmacies, and any business where customers ask the same questions before deciding.

Setup takes about a week. You write your product and service details in plain language — we build the chatbot's knowledge base from that. No app download needed. It lives on WhatsApp, where your customers already are.

The goal isn't to replace your team. It's to give them back the time that's currently going to repetitive questions — so it goes to the work only people can do.
