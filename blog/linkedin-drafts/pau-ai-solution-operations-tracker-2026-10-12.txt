You close the shop at night, open the books, and spend 40 minutes reconciling cash and stock — every single day.

For most Malaysian SME owners running a café, minimarket, or retail outlet, daily operations tracking is the job they do after the real job. By the time the numbers are ready, it's midnight.

The Pau AI Operations Tracker changes that.

It gives you a single place where your daily numbers come together — cash takings, stock movements, staff attendance, and daily sales target progress — without you having to pull them from three different apps or a WhatsApp group.

Here's what it handles:

✅ Daily sales vs target: tracks progress in real time so you know by 3pm whether you're on pace or falling short
✅ Stock movement alerts: flags items that have dropped below reorder level during the day, not at the end of the week
✅ Cash reconciliation: logs cash, card, and e-wallet takings against POS records so discrepancies surface immediately

It's built for F&B operators, minimarket owners, and service businesses who need a daily pulse on their numbers — without hiring a finance person to deliver it.
