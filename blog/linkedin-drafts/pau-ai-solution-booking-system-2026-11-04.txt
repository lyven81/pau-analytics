You missed three booking calls today. Each one was a customer who gave up and called someone else.

Service businesses — salons, clinics, tuition centres, workshops — lose bookings every day not because they are bad at what they do, but because their booking process requires a human to respond. Customers message at 9pm. They WhatsApp during lunch. They look you up on a Sunday afternoon. If nobody answers, they move on.

The Pau AI Booking System handles the full booking flow automatically.

A customer messages your WhatsApp at 10pm asking for a slot next Friday afternoon. The system checks your live calendar, finds what is available, applies your pricing rules for peak hours and public holidays, and sends a confirmed booking via WhatsApp — in under a minute. No missed message. No morning catch-up. Just a confirmed booking when you open your phone.

What it manages without you:

Real-time calendar checks so double-bookings do not happen. Peak-hour and off-peak pricing applied automatically. Instant booking confirmations sent via WhatsApp. Day-before reminders to reduce no-shows. Waitlist management when your preferred slots are full.

The system works for any appointment-based service business: dental clinics, physiotherapy centres, tutoring, beauty treatments, car workshops, and more.

Most businesses using it recover between 3 and 7 lost bookings per week — bookings that came in outside of business hours and previously went unanswered.
