Case Study: How a Manufacturer Found Its Best Resellers
Published: 27 July 2026

---

You reward your best-selling resellers with bigger discounts and more support.

But what if your top-selling resellers are the ones losing you money?

A Malaysian biscuits manufacturer analysed 60,855 orders placed by 635 resellers over 26 months. What they found stopped them cold.

The reseller receiving the highest discount in the entire network? Gross margin: negative $1,250.

More than half of the top 15 discount recipients generated negative returns. The company was spending the most on the partners costing them the most.

Meanwhile, just 65 resellers — 10% of the network — were quietly carrying the majority of gross margin. One partner alone contributed $182,884 in gross margin at an 18% margin rate.

The remaining 460 resellers (72%) contributed almost nothing to profit, while still consuming attention, logistics, and support resources.

The problem was not the resellers. The problem was the metric.

Revenue looks impressive in a spreadsheet. Margin tells you who is actually worth investing in.

If you manage a reseller or distributor network, ask yourself one question today: which of your top-revenue partners actually has the strongest margin contribution?

The answer might completely change where your discounts and support go next.

Check your margins, not just your totals.
