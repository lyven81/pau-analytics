FRIDAY — Pau AI Use Case Spotlight
Category: analytics-agent / retail chain
Published: 19 September 2026

---

Wei Jian runs a clothing retail chain with four outlets across the Klang Valley. Each outlet has its own manager, its own stock, and its own weekly sales rhythm.

Every Monday morning, he called each manager for a verbal update. By the time he had spoken to all four, two hours were gone and he still had no clear picture of which outlet needed attention and why.

He started using Pau AI's analytics agent, connected to his weekly sales exports from all four outlets. The first week, he asked three questions before his morning coffee:

- "Which outlet had the lowest revenue per square foot this week?" — Outlet 3 in Kepong, consistently for the past six weeks
- "What are the top 5 products by sell-through rate across all outlets this month?" — Three were the same at every outlet; two were outlet-specific bestsellers that no one had noticed
- "Which outlet has the widest gap between weekend and weekday sales?" — Outlet 2 in Subang, where weekday traffic was less than a third of weekend volume

Based on that third answer, Wei Jian shifted one staff member from a weekday shift at Outlet 2 to a weekend shift at Outlet 4, where weekend demand was outrunning capacity. No new hires. Just reallocation.

Within a month, Outlet 4's weekend conversion rate improved and Outlet 2's staffing cost per transaction dropped.

What changed was not the business. What changed was how fast he could ask the right question.
