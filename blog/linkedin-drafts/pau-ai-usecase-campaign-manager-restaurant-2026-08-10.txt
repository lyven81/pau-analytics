FRIDAY — Pau AI Use Case Spotlight
Category: campaign-manager / restaurant
Published: 10 August 2026

---

Danny runs a Cantonese restaurant in Subang Jaya. Lunch is steady. Friday evenings fill up on their own. Tuesday and Wednesday nights are another story.

He had been sending WhatsApp promotions to his full contact list — 650 people — every few weeks. The response was inconsistent. Some nights it helped; most nights it didn't. He had no idea whether the right customers were even seeing the offers.

He started using Pau AI's campaign manager.

For his next Tuesday promotion, the AI sorted his contacts by visit history: lapsed customers who hadn't come in for 30 or more days, previous weeknight dinner visitors, and first-time lunch customers who hadn't tried the dinner menu.

He sent a simple offer — a complimentary soup for tables of four, Tuesday only — to 180 targeted contacts.

The AI tracked:
- Which segment responded and within what timeframe
- What each table ordered and their average spend
- How campaign revenue compared to the cost of the offer

Nine tables came in that Tuesday. Six were lapsed customers. The complimentary soup cost RM126. Total food orders: RM1,240.

More useful than the revenue figure was what Danny learned: his lapsed customers responded better than any other segment when offered something specific to that evening. His Tuesday campaigns are now built around that group every time.

Slow nights are no longer a guess. They are a campaign away.
