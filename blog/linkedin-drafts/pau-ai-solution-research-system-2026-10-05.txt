You know your product range. You do not always know what is happening around it.

Supplier price lists change without warning. A competitor runs a promotion you hear about three days later. A new product from China floods the market and undercuts your bestseller before you have had time to respond.

Most business owners find out about these things from customers, from other traders, or by accident. By then, the margin damage is already done.

Pau AI's research system changes how you track the market. Connect it to the data sources that matter — supplier catalogues, competitor websites, industry news, wholesale price feeds — and it monitors them on your behalf.

- Ask it: "Has my palm oil supplier changed their bulk pricing this month?"
- Ask it: "What promotions are the two minimarkets near my outlets running right now?"
- Ask it: "Are there new instant noodle brands entering the market at a lower price point?"

It does not just retrieve information. It compares it to what you already know — flags the changes, surfaces the gaps, and tells you what is worth paying attention to.

One hardware retailer in Shah Alam used it to track competitor pricing on PVC pipes and wiring. He had been losing quotes to a rival on three product lines without knowing the competitor had dropped prices six weeks earlier. Once he could see pricing movements in near-real time, he adjusted and recovered the contracts.

Built for retailers, importers, and F&B operators who make decisions based on market conditions — and need to know when those conditions change.
