You have RM50,000 to spend on new stock this quarter.

How do you decide what to bring in?

Most SME owners go by gut feel. What sold well last season. What a supplier recommended. What looked popular on TikTok. It is not market research — it is educated guessing with a large budget.

Pau AI's research system gives you a way to actually check before you commit.

- "What products in this category are picking up traction in Malaysia right now?" — see what customers are responding to before you place the order

- "What are competitors charging for this in my area?" — make sure your pricing is in range before the first unit arrives

It works for retail buyers deciding what to stock, F&B operators planning a menu update, and service businesses evaluating whether to add a new offering.
