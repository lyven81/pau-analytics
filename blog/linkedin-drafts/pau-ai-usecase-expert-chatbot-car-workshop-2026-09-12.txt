Uncle Danny runs a car workshop in Subang. Twenty years of experience. Three mechanics. One phone that rings all day with the same questions.

"My car is making a noise when I brake — what is that?" "How much does it cost to change brake pads?" "Can you service a 2019 Perodua Bezza?" "How long will it take?" His mechanics know every answer. But they are under a car when the calls come in, and by the time someone picks up the phone, the caller has already moved on to the next workshop on the list.

Uncle Danny was losing customers not because his prices were wrong or his work was bad. He was losing them because he could not answer fast enough.

Now the expert chatbot handles the common questions while his team handles the cars:

- Tells customers whether their symptoms sound like brake wear, suspension, or transmission issues — and what the typical repair involves
- Quotes standard service prices: RM180 for a full engine service on a Myvi, RM95 for brake pads per axle, RM45 for tyre rotation
- Confirms whether a car model is within their service scope and what parts need to be ordered in advance
- Lets customers know realistic turnaround times: same-day for basic services, next-day for parts that need ordering

Uncle Danny's phone still rings. But the calls that come through now are from customers ready to make an appointment — not from people asking if he services their car.
