David runs a specialty imported snacks shop in Subang Jaya. Three times a year, he places orders from suppliers in Japan, Korea, and Taiwan. Each order ties up RM30,000 to RM50,000 in stock.

The problem: he never really knew what would sell.

He ordered what his supplier pushed. He went by last year's bestsellers. Half the time, slow-moving products ended up on the discount rack for months, eroding the margin on everything else.

He tried Pau AI's research system before his most recent order. Three questions changed how he bought.

- "Which Korean snack brands are picking up traction in Malaysian online communities right now?" — two specific brands kept appearing; neither had been in his previous order
- "What are Lazada and Shopee sellers charging for this product in West Malaysia?" — he discovered he was pricing RM3 above the market average on one of his staples
- "What do Malaysian buyers say about matcha snack products after purchase?" — freshness complaints and requests for resealable packaging came up repeatedly

He adjusted his order: two new products added, one price corrected, and a note sent to his supplier about packaging specs.

His next quarter? The lowest unsold stock he had seen in two years.

He did not guess. He checked.
