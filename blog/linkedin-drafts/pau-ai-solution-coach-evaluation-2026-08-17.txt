Solution: Coach Evaluation System

You have three staff members whose clients keep coming back.

You have two whose clients quietly stop showing up.

You probably have a feeling about which is which. But feelings are not a retention strategy.

Service businesses — fitness studios, coaching centres, clinics, salons — live or die by individual staff performance. When one person on your team is quietly losing clients, you typically find out two things too late: after the clients are gone, and after the reviews reflect it.

The Pau AI Coach Evaluation System tracks performance continuously, not just at annual reviews.

Here is what it monitors across your team:

Client retention rate per coach or therapist, updated monthly. Session engagement patterns — who is booking consistently and who is drifting. Progress indicators linked to the service you deliver, whether that is fitness milestones, learning outcomes, or satisfaction scores.

When a coach's numbers start slipping, the system flags it early — not when the client cancels, but three to four months before, when the pattern first appears in the data.

You can still have the conversation that changes the outcome.

Fitness studios, professional coaching businesses, and service centres across Malaysia use this to develop their teams before problems compound into losses.
