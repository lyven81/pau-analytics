Your GrabFood orders are worth RM43. Your dine-in orders are worth RM31.

Which channel is actually making you more money?

We analysed 12 months of orders for a nasi lemak restaurant in Petaling Jaya — 18,400 transactions across dine-in, GrabFood, and WhatsApp bulk orders. The delivery AOV looked great on paper.

Then we subtracted the 30% platform commission. And the RM4.80 average packaging cost per order.

GrabFood net revenue per transaction: RM25.30.
Dine-in net revenue per transaction: RM31.

For every RM100 of GrabFood revenue showing on the books, the owner was keeping RM59.

There was a menu problem too. He had 34 items listed on GrabFood. Six of them drove 72% of all delivery orders. The other 28 items contributed 28% of orders — and 67% of all the 1-star and 2-star reviews.

Four changes. Three months. Delivery net margin up 14%. Dine-in weekend revenue up 11%.
