Your customer sent a WhatsApp message at 9pm asking about your price and availability. You saw it at 7am. By then, they had already booked somewhere else.

This happens every single day for small businesses in Malaysia. Not because the owner doesn't care — but because there are only so many hours in a day, and replying to enquiries at night is not how most people want to spend them.

Pau AI's WhatsApp agent replies for you. Not with a holding message that says "we will get back to you" — with an actual answer. It knows your services, your prices, your slots, and your hours. When a customer asks "do you have space this Friday evening?", it checks and responds in seconds.

Where it matters most: evenings and weekends. That is when customers browse, compare, and decide — and when most small businesses go dark. An agent that answers at 10pm, accurately and in your tone, keeps the conversation alive instead of losing it.

One specific thing it handles well: price enquiries. Customers who ask prices via WhatsApp are close to a decision. A fast, accurate answer converts. A three-hour wait does not.

It runs over WhatsApp. No new app for your customers. No separate platform for you to learn.

Works well for: restaurants, clinics, service providers, tutors, fitness studios, and any business where customers ask questions before booking or buying.
